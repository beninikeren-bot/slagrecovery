Three paths

How to verify the material

A

Send the analyses your plant already has.

B

Run the tests in your own laboratory — to the programme we provide.

In both cases the material stays with you.

C

If your own data is not sufficient, send a sample. Testing is carried out by an independent laboratory to our programme.

What to include

An order of magnitude is enough

Exact figures are not required.

  • What production it comes from and what the material is.
  • How much has accumulated and whether it is still being generated.
  • Who owns the dump and who controls it.
  • What you want to achieve: free up land, recover metal, reduce environmental exposure.
  • Any existing analyses, with the year and the sampling zone noted.
