Industry questions

Processing wet sludge

Wet sludge requires a mandatory dewatering stage before it is returned to production or separated. Iron-bearing gas cleaning sludges contain a significant share of iron, which makes them a valuable charge component.

Moisture is removed with vacuum filters, filter presses or screw centrifuges. Using drying beds for natural evaporation carries a risk of groundwater contamination with heavy metals. In steelmaking sludges, zinc content must be controlled so that it does not accumulate in the sinter and blast furnace circuit.

Ferroalloy dusts and sludges must be pelletised or briquetted before reuse in electric furnaces. The processing route for sludges is always case-specific.

R. Keren has a worked-out processing route and engineering package for one class of material — silicomanganese slag. For the other materials mentioned here, the route requires adaptation and separate laboratory confirmation.
