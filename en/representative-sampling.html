Rules for taking a representative sample

A sampling error is not corrected by the quality of the laboratory: a test result cannot be more accurate than the sample. A dump is not homogeneous, so the sample is taken from several zones distributed across the area. Sampling is done below the weathered layer, because the surface is oxidised and washed out by precipitation.

The procedure combines point samples and reduces them by coning and quartering to the mass set by the test programme. Coarse metal inclusions are not discarded, otherwise the final recovery calculation is distorted.

A sealed reference portion of the sample must be formed and kept in storage at the plant. This preserves the possibility of an arbitration check should a dispute arise. Sampling is a one-off event that determines the accuracy of the entire project.

R. Keren has a worked-out processing route and engineering package for one class of material — silicomanganese slag. For the other materials mentioned here, the route requires adaptation and separate laboratory confirmation.
