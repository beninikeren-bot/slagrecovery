Assessing a dump: the tests you need

Assessing a dump is built on a combination of physical and chemical test methods. Particle size distribution, metallic inclusion content, strength and bulk density are mandatory. Elevated true density is an indirect indication that a metallic phase is present in ferroalloy waste.

Copper slags require phase analysis — microscopy and X-ray diffraction — to distinguish sulphide copper from oxide copper. Rapid composition checks are possible with portable analysers, but their readings are preliminary.

A typical mistake is to look only at chemical composition and disregard the heterogeneity of the dump. We begin with an analysis of your material, carried out by an independent laboratory to our programme.

R. Keren works with all secondary metallurgical materials. The processing route is determined by the properties of the specific material and confirmed in the laboratory during project work — a working step, not a restriction on the range of materials.
