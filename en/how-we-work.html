Order of work

How we work

We do not start with equipment.

Not this: “We have a crusher, a magnetic separator and a press — let us build a plant out of them.”

But this: “You have a material. First let us establish what can be done with it.”

Equipment, integration and the plant come only after that.

Step 1
You send data on the material

Four questions need answers:

  • What production is it from and what material is it?
  • How much has accumulated, and is it still being generated?
  • Who controls this material?
  • What do you want to achieve: free up land, recover metal, reduce environmental exposure?

Exact figures are not required at this step — an order of magnitude is enough.

Step 2
Preliminary assessment

Using the data you send and the analyses you already have, we look at whether there is a case for going further. If the material is clearly unsuitable, we say so straight away.

Step 3
Testing

If the data is not sufficient for a decision, there are three ways to obtain it: send the analyses your plant already has; run the tests in your own laboratory to a programme we write for your material; or send a sample — an independent laboratory carries out the tests to the same programme.

We have no laboratory of our own. With the first two options the material stays with you.

Step 4
Economic assessment

Based on the test results, we assess whether processing is economically justified. If it is not, we say so directly and stop there. We do not promise in advance that processing will be profitable: that is the subject of the assessment, not a starting assumption.

Step 5
Solution and engineering

If the economics hold, we develop the technical solution: the process route, the equipment list, the plant layout. Equipment is selected to suit your material, not the other way round — we are not tied to particular manufacturers.

Step 6
Implementation

We coordinate equipment supply and the work on site. We have no design institute of our own: the sections of documentation that require a local signature under the laws of your country are prepared by an organisation in the country of the project — your own designer, a plant contractor, or an organisation engaged for the project.

Principle

After each step, the decision is yours

We do not run the whole route in one go. After each step you decide whether to continue. Major investment comes only after confirmed data.

For every parameter it is visible whether it is confirmed by a document and a test or still an assumption. You can see exactly what each decision rests on.
